In August 2026, SuperB co-founders Pierre, Bowen and Dino joined the startup podcast *創業新聲帶* (S03EP05) to talk about the thinking behind DOGTOR.

## What we talked about

- **How AI helps students find what they truly don't understand**: starting from answer records and knowledge points, so practice returns to the concepts that need work.
- **Game-like design and a companion character**: why DOGTOR has a studious corgi, and how playfulness makes students want to open the app every day.
- **Learning reports for parents**: helping parents understand how their children are doing — and how to support them next.
