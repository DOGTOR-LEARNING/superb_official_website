Open any social app and it's easy to save a café, an exhibition or a hiking trail. Look back at your saved list, though, and very few of those places have actually been visited. MapIt starts from this everyday gap.

> This is a log of research still in progress. What follows describes our direction and early observations — not proven findings.

## What we want to understand

- Why do people save places, and what are they thinking when they do?
- What gets in the way between saving and actually going?
- When friends decide where to go together, why do discussions so often go nowhere?

## Where we're exploring

MapIt is a National Taiwan University team capstone. We're trying to bring scattered saves onto a single map and design a flow where friends browse together and share preferences.

We're also exploring the role of AI: should it organize information and suggest options, or push more actively toward a decision? Our current hypothesis is that AI works better as an assistant that reduces decision fatigue than as something that decides for people — but this still needs more validation.

## What remains to be tested

Our observations so far come from limited interviews and prototype tests; neither the sample nor the contexts allow generalization. We'll share more once the research makes clearer progress.
